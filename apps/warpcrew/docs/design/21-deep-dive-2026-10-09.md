# Warp Crew deep dive and roadmap proposal (2026-10-09)

Status: **proposal, awaiting Garrett's answers** (questions at the end). Nothing here is approved.
Source: `main@721061b`. Method: a fresh-save phone-size playthrough (390×844) through the tutorial, first fight,
hub, crew and contract board, plus three read-only code audits (crew and gacha; progression, economy and shop;
content, story and live-ops). File references are relative to `apps/warpcrew/`.

## The verdict in one paragraph

The foundations are better than most mobile games at this stage: verified purchases, cloud saves and
subscriptions on game-core, a real FTL-style crew fight, a handsome ship cutaway, a strong splash and captain
portraits, and a dry, funny writing voice. What is missing is the game around them. **Mercenaries barely matter
in fights**, so the gacha has nothing to sell. **There is very little content** and no main story or endgame.
**Rewards don't feel like rewards**: no pull animation, no purchase unboxing, no music. And **the daily loops pay
nothing**. The work below fixes these in order of impact.

## What is strong (keep and build on)

- Platform: game-core port done and audited; idempotent server grants; one-time packs; subscription with trial
  and cancel-save; deterministic sims and about 90 test files.
- Fight: FTL-lite real-time rooms, shields, fires, boarders, crew drag. Rare on mobile and a genuine hook. The
  guided first fight was clear and short.
- Art: the splash and the painted captain portraits are the "AAA retro" look. The Sparrow cutaway and the six
  enemy cutaways are good.
- Writing: "The clerks are polite, slow and armed. Your papers are mostly real." Keep this voice everywhere.
- Fairness rules (approved 2026-09-21): transparent odds, pity, a deterministic path to a chosen merc, nothing
  permanently paid-only. These are also what keeps a gacha trusted (and legal in more markets).

## The problems, ranked by impact

### 1. Mercenaries are stat sticks, and the fight ignores them

- Each merc is one number: base power plus one bonus picked by role (`src/data/crewRoster.js`). There are no
  abilities, traits or ultimates.
- In the real-time fights a merc counts only by role. Manning a room gives ×1.15, or ×1.3 on a role match
  (`src/systems/ftlCombat.js:191-196`). Power, rarity and bonus are ignored. Medics, traders and scouts have no
  fight job at all (`src/systems/stations.js:4-9`).
- Some bonuses are inert. "Crit" never crits; it only feeds the pre-fight threat number
  (`src/systems/passives.js:52`). "Fuel cut" does nothing on contracts (`src/systems/contracts.js:162,252`).
- Enemy strength is pulled up to 82% of any power above its listed level (`src/systems/combat.js:361-367`), and
  walls never drop below threat 1.2. Collecting stronger crew barely changes outcomes.
- Result: a Legendary feels the same as a Common. The core spending loop of a hero collector (pull, feel
  stronger, push further) does not exist yet.

### 2. Not enough content, and no structure

- Contracts: 3 profiles × 3 titles × 3 briefs, 3 offers a day, every one the same 2–3 beat run
  (`src/data/contracts.js:4-36`).
- 29 travel events, 34 away sites, 51 beacons in 5 sectors, 30 story beats of about 15 words each, and about
  20 minutes of total reading.
- Every enemy is the same 4-room ship whose loadout depends only on a threat number. Faction flavour ("moves when
  you look away") has no mechanic behind it.
- No main campaign, quest chains or recurring characters. Crew bios and quotes are written but **never shown**.
- No endgame. The last lines are "End of the mapped war" and "Apex space. Later." (`src/data/galaxies.js:274-276`).

### 3. Rewards don't feel rewarding

- A normal pull is a toast. A 10-pull is "N rare+" and three names. Purchases show "Purchase applied" with the
  generic coin click (`src/main.js:607-648, 1091-1098`).
- No rarity fanfare, level-up moment, currency count-up or item fly-in. The same coin sound plays for everything.
- The shop is text rows with price buttons: no product art, value badges or sale frames
  (`src/ui/bridge.js:1651-1764`).
- **No music at all**, only 35 sound effects.

### 4. Retention loops pay nothing

- The daily plan (3 tasks) and the week goals (4) give **no reward**. The 7-day login does not escalate and has
  no calendar screen.
- No idle income ("Idle Starship" with nothing accruing while away): station output is display only
  (`src/systems/stations.js:1-2`).
- No events, seasons, pass, achievements, leaderboards or inbox. The core has all of these, switched off in
  `apps/server/games/warpcrew/game.config.ts:41-52`.

### 5. The economy stalls free players

- The second Siege wall (Veil) was not broken in 30 days in any of the 15 simulated runs: a 15–25 day stall
  (`docs/qa/2026-09-22-encounter-balance-evidence.md`).
- Free gem income is about 80 a month, so hull tiers 3–9 are effectively paid-only (the Dreadnought is about $200
  of gems).
- Medals pile up (441–1,024 unspent at day 30) because nothing in the sim spends them.
- The day resets at two different times: UTC for login, free hire and subscription; local midnight for the
  contract board and siege damage.
- Reputation plus permanent Luck inflate rarity: by mid-game a Legendary or better arrives about every 9 pulls,
  and only 3 mercs sit above Legendary, so the chase runs out.

### 6. Polish bugs a player sees in the first ten minutes

- **8 of the 46 merc portraits have a hot-pink background baked in**: Tink (the guaranteed welcome recruit),
  Brink, Greaves, Kal, Moth, NUB-4, Oso and Yara (`public/art/pixel/crew/*.png`).
- **The four captain templates are in the gacha's Common pool** (`src/systems/gacha.js:112`). My first free daily
  hire was a copy of my own captain.
- Two portrait styles clash: painted HD pixel art (splash, captains) next to flat pixel art (Bolt, Tink).
- Captains are generic ("Cyborg", "Human", "Common · Power 10"), not characters.
- Developer language reaches players: "Soft-launch prestige hull. Real war deck." (`src/data/ships.js:80`); the
  Log tab roadmap with "LiveOps" and "Mid-core" (`src/data/intel.js`); fight tells still describe the old
  Brace/Burn/Board orders (`src/systems/combat.js:173-185`, shown in `src/ui/contractView.js:147,162`).
- Stat-sheet UI: "Helm 100 (+0)", "Shields 100 (-10)", "2–3 beats", "Normal fuel 2F".
- Crew on the hub are about 20 px tall and share one recoloured body per species.

### 7. Money safety before real-money gacha

- Pulls roll on the device (`Math.random`), and gem spends happen in the client-owned save blob. That is fine
  for a single-player beta but not for real-money pulls, limited banners or leaderboards. Move premium spends and
  pulls to server actions (the core already has grants) before launch.
- CI runs only game-core's checks, not the Warp Crew suites.

## The proposal: what "kick-ass" means for this game

**Promise:** build the strangest, most capable mercenary crew in a frontier being eaten by the Eclipse Swarm,
and turn a battered Sparrow into a legend, five great minutes at a time, with endless depth for those who
want it.

### Mercenaries (the heart of the game and of the money)

- **Every merc gets a kit.** A passive that bends a fight rule, plus an active ability you tap on their portrait
  mid-fight (on a cooldown), plus one signature line. Example: Jen, *Overdrive*: her room's next volley fires
  twice. Bolt, *Patch Job*: instantly repair 1 system bar.
- **Rarity is how much a kit changes play**, not a bigger number. Common: one simple passive. Rare: passive plus
  active. Epic: plus a synergy tag. Legendary and above: rule-breakers (Wisp phases through walls to fix any room;
  Voidwake resets the enemy's weapon charge).
- **Synergy tags** from the 23 factions and origins already in the data: 2/3/4 of a kind give squad bonuses.
  This gives collection a reason to exist.
- **Deeper progression:** Level (medals), Stars (duplicates or universal shards; duplicates always keep value),
  Ascension at 5★ (Veteran → Elite → Legend: ability upgrades and a cosmetic aura), and later signature gear
  from events.
- **Bonds:** crew who fly together unlock short scenes and a personal loyalty mission. The written bios finally
  get shown, and every merc becomes a story.
- **Gacha structure:** a standard banner, a featured rate-up banner rotating every 2 weeks, and limited event
  banners that rerun later. Every pull earns a mark, and 200 marks buys the featured merc (the approved
  deterministic path). Odds and pity shown in the game. Cap or remove the reputation/Luck rarity inflation so
  Legendaries stay special. Pulls run on the server.
- **The reveal:** drop pods or beacon flares with rarity colours (blue, purple, gold, prismatic), a 10-pull
  results grid, a "priority transmission" sting and full-screen portrait for Legendary and above, and the merc's
  signature line.
- **Fix the fight math** so crew strength reads on screen. Soften the 82% enemy pull-up and let power, rarity and
  abilities move win rates. Give medics, scouts and traders fight jobs (heal, reveal enemy rooms or dodge,
  better salvage).

### The ship (the long grind)

- **Hulls that look and fight differently:** each hull tier gets its own cutaway with more rooms and slots (start
  with 3–4 cutaways: Sparrow, Corvette, Frigate, Cruiser).
- **Drydock milestones every 5 levels:** a new tier name, a visible room change and a perk choice, plus a second
  drydock slot (earnable, or a purchase).
- **Weapon loot:** weapons drop from fights with rarity, upgrade levels and modules crafted from salvage. Finding
  a new gun is FTL's best feeling.
- **Cosmetics:** hull paints, engine trails, room themes and crew outfits, for high-end spending without breaking
  balance.

### World, story and endless things to do

- **A main campaign:** ordered chapters across the sectors, each with an arc, recurring characters (a broker, a
  rival captain, the Swarm's herald), set-piece missions and boss fights with their own mechanics, told in 2–3
  panel transmissions with portraits.
- **A contract generator:** client × cargo × twist × place × enemy faction gives hundreds of combinations. Add
  mission types with real rule changes: escort, blockade run, salvage under a timer, bounty, defend waves,
  smuggling past scans.
- **Enemy factions that play differently:** pirates board, Swarm drones swarm, Veil wraiths cloak, Ice Raiders
  use ions, Wardens stack shields. Add elites and modifiers.
- **The Rift:** an endless procedural sector with rising modifiers and a weekly leaderboard; the endgame for
  engaged players.
- **A codex:** discoveries, enemy dossiers and crew stories unlock as you play.

### Live-ops and seasons

- **Seasons of 4–6 weeks**, each with a theme ("Season 1: The Ember Uprising"), a limited event map, an event merc
  earnable on the free track, an event currency and an event shop.
- **A season pass** (free track plus premium track at about $9.99): the biggest single lever in this genre.
- **Weekly rotations:** double-salvage weekends, boss rush, a Swarm incursion with a community goal.
- Turn on the core's live-ops windows, leaderboards, inbox and achievements.

### Retention

- **Idle income:** stations earn credits and salvage while you are away, capped by cargo storage (upgrading the
  cap is a grind and a sink). A "welcome back, Captain" claim screen.
- **A 28-day login calendar** that escalates to a merc on day 28.
- **Daily orders** worth points toward a daily chest, plus a weekly chest.
- Achievements with gem rewards, a comeback bundle, and Jest notifications for timers and events.
- One reset time for everything.

### Monetization (on top of what exists)

- Season pass; featured banners with spark; first-purchase double gems per pack.
- Daily and weekly deal rotations; chapter milestone packs; featured-merc bundles.
- Second drydock slot; cosmetics; a weekly "supply drop" subscription next to the monthly Commission.
- The universal reward reveal on every purchase: items fly into the wallet, counters roll up, the sound matches
  the size of the reward.
- Keep the approved fairness rules: they are what makes heavy spenders trust the game.

### Feel, art and sound (the "AAA retro" bar)

- **One reward-reveal system** reused for pulls, purchases, chests, level-ups and unlocks.
- Juice: hit-stop, screen shake, explosion sprites, damage numbers, salvage flying to the wallet.
- **Music:** a synth or chiptune soundtrack (hub, fight, boss, event themes). Today there is none.
- **One portrait style:** move all 50 portraits to the painted HD-pixel look of the splash and captains, with
  bespoke ship sprites for Epic and above. Named captains with backstories.
- **Visual UI:** icons and before/after bars instead of "Helm 100 (+0)", and bigger crew on the hub.
- **Copy:** remove developer language; reuse the event voice in every card.

## Proposed order

| Phase | What | Why first |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Quick fixes (days) | Pink portraits; captains out of the gacha pool; dev text out; stale fight tells; one reset time; Warp Crew suites in CI | Visible in the first 10 minutes; cheap |
| 1. Crew matter | Merc kits and active abilities in fights, role jobs for all 7 roles, fight-math fix, synergy tags, the reveal, featured banner with spark and odds, server-side pulls | The heart of fun *and* money; everything else sells crew or ship |
| 2. Reward feel and retention | Universal reward reveal, shop redesign, music and SFX pass, idle income, login calendar, daily and weekly chests, achievements | Makes every session feel good and pays the daily loop |
| 3. World | Campaign chapters 1–2 with transmissions, contract generator, faction enemy mechanics, bonds and loyalty missions, codex | Endless things to do and a reason to care |
| 4. Live | Season 1, season pass, event map and merc, leaderboards, The Rift | Long-term retention and the biggest revenue lever |
| 5. Ship grind | Hull cutaways, weapon loot and tiers, modules, drydock milestones, cosmetics | The months-long chase (art-heavy, so it overlaps with art budget timing) |

Stage 4 of the port (staging on Railway, a phone test inside Jest) can run in parallel at any time; it only
needs Garrett's console and Railway steps.
