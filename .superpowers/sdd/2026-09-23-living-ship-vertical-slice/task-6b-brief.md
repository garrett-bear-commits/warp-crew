## Scope and invariants

- Own `src/systems/encounterState.js`, `test/encounter_session.test.mjs`, and the minimal changes needed in `player.js`, `sessionLoop.js`, `bridge.js`/`contractView.js`, `combatView.js`, `main.js`, `contracts.js`, `contractState.js` or `contractRewards.js`. Keep existing script-3 in-progress fights and legacy risky/strange encounters on their old resolver; do not reinterpret a saved contract with no `activeEncounter` as a new fight. New guided script-4 distress (when Task 7 creates it) and one fixed reliable `pirate_scout` path get the new reducer. If source paths do not offer a reliable `pirate_scout` encounter, state that as a blocker rather than inventing a route/reward.
- Encounter identity is `{acceptanceId, revision}`. `applyEncounterAction(player,{acceptanceId,revision,order})` rejects stale identity, stale revision, terminal state, and invalid order without changing player. A legal beat commits a new encounter snapshot; recompute availability-adjusted station outputs from committed crew status at that beat and update only the appropriate output snapshot. No wallet/fuel/reputation grant in a beat.
- Launch/route fuel remains paid exactly once by existing contract actions. A winning terminal beat creates a valid `activeContract.result`, sets stage `return`, and retains the terminal encounter through reload until claim. Only `claimContractReward` grants route rewards, then clears the encounter. A normal loss presents reason and `encounter-recover`; recovery clears the failed contract/encounter with no reward and ship hull at least 1. Preserve existing payout provenance and avoid a second randomized combat roll. If legacy payout helpers are reused, separate reward construction from old chance/hull mutation; do not call the old resolver merely to force a win.
- Versioned encounter normalization on load must verify snapshot and contract acceptance identity. Corrupt snapshots should fail safe without fuel refund or reward duplication and should not erase valid script-3 contracts. Add recovery event/instruction if needed. Save/restore through the current durable `persistSessionTransition` boundary before animations, including on each beat and on claim/recover. Do not derive authority from animations or unsaved UI state.
- Render a compact threat target/countdown, current player/enemy hull/shield/system state, station output effects, honest order costs/availability/reasons/cooldowns, and an obvious no-order/advance beat action. Do not show legacy chance cards for active new encounters. On a terminal win, show claim; on loss, show recover. Animation is optional feedback; state and controls must work with reduced motion and after reload. Existing camera pan/zoom must stay enabled during combat.
