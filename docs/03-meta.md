# Meta & Economy

## Currencies
| ID | Name | Role |
|----|------|------|
| credits | Credits | Soft progression |
| fuel | Fuel | Energy (1/hour default) |
| gems | Gems | Premium |
| medals | Medals | Crew leveling |
| reputation | Reputation | Gacha quality + story standing |

## Ships
- Sparrow (starter): 2→4 crew slots, forever upgradable
- Corvette: 6 slots, credits grind or gems early

## Crew
Mercenaries via gacha; hire/dismiss freely; Medals to level; locked while on expedition.

## Monetization (Jest)
Fuel packs, starter bundle, gacha (gems), premium ships, battle pass, expedition skips, light VIP (daily fuel + regen).
