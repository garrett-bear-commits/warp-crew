QA checklist

  • Board ship from the full-screen crew splash.
  • Send Bolt to Shields, answer the distress call, and use Brace.
  • Watch the crew win, bring the cargo aboard, and name the ship.
  • Meet your guaranteed-Uncommon free recruit; sign-in can be skipped in this preview.
  • From the ship, open the next job. Try panning, pinching, Focus, and +/− zoom.
  • Tell us what felt confusing, slow, or satisfying, and where you stopped.
